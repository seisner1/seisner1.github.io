I am a current postdoctoral scholar at Oregon State University, working in The Phytoplankton Ecophysiology Col(Lab)oration with [Michael J. Behrenfeld](https://bpp.oregonstate.edu/users/michael-behrenfeld). My research interests are primarily in ocean-ecosystem interactions - namely how phytoplankton communities respond to changes in the physical environment. I am currently working to model phytoplankton community properties, such as biomass, chl:c ratios, division and loss rates, and net primary production by combining satellite bio-optical fields (primarily from the new PACE-OCI instrument) with ecological and physiologial theory. I am also interested in identifying commanalities in how phytoplankton communities respond to environmental forcing across biogeographical regions.
